## 1. Overall Revision Strategy

**Core flip**: every environment moves from *Teacher → Student* to **Student → Teacher**.

The lesson should feel like a **6-stop explorer rally**, not a slideshow of explanations. For each environment, students should:

1. **Observe** an image / clip (10s, no talking)
2. **Choose** A or B (either/or, body votes)
3. **Act out** one short physical move (10s)
4. **Say one sentence** (sentence frame)
5. **Earn 1 ⭐** (whole-class or individual sticker)

Teacher's role shifts from explainer → mission director.

### Star reward system

Add a **6-star badge** that fills as the class progresses through the 6 environments. Possible implementations:

- **Whole-class chart** on the whiteboard: 6 empty stars, color in 1 after each environment is complete
- **Individual passport stickers**: each child has a "Day 1 Passport" with 6 stars to stamp
- **Class trophy badge slide** at the end (the existing "Day 1 Badge" slide can be enhanced to show all 6 stars filled in)

> **Slide adjustment**: On the existing "🎒 6 个任务  6 Missions" overview slide, change `完成 → 拿 ⭐` to `每完成一个 → 拿 1 ⭐ (一共 6 颗!)` and add a 6-star tracker bar at the bottom.

### Pacing principle

Compress each environment from 5 slides (4 aspects + 1 interaction) down to a felt experience of **one short challenge per env** by treating the 4 aspect slides as a teacher resource (notes / printable / station cards) and using **only the interaction slide for whole-class delivery**. The aspect slides remain as backup but are not the primary frame.

### Language gradient

Each frame appears in 2 levels on screen — let the teacher cue which level a child uses based on their grade. Keep Chinese primary, English under-text only as a teacher hint.

- Always do the move 1× as a teacher demo, then "全班一起!"
- Keep moves to 10–30 sec — long enough to break the slide, short enough to refocus
- Use a clap (3 claps) to signal "stop and freeze"

## 6. Active Review Game Improvements

### 🛡️ 安全 vs 危险 Safe vs Dangerous (slide 42)

**Current**: students respond from their seats.

**Upgrade — make it a STANDING game**:
1. Whole class **STANDS UP** at start
2. Teacher reads a situation (use the 6 examples already on the slide, or new ones below)
3. Students respond with body:
   - ✅ **安全 = 双手举高 V 形 + 站直**
   - ❌ **危险 = 双手在胸前交叉 X + 蹲下**
4. Wrong answer → sit out for 1 round (reset on next "Safe" answer to keep everyone engaged)
5. Run 8–10 prompts in 4 minutes

**New prompts to add** (mix into existing 6):
- 「下大雨时, 站在大树下避雨」 → ❌
- 「看到不认识的果子, 尝一口」 → ❌
- 「迷路了, 留在原地等大人」 → ✅
- 「在河边一个人下水玩」 → ❌
- 「热天出门戴帽子 + 多带水」 → ✅
- 「看到陌生的狗, 慢慢走开」 → ✅

**Sentence stretch (G1–3)**: After each X answer, ask one student to say 「___ 不安全, 因为 ___」.

### 🔍 我在哪里? Where Am I? (slide 43)

**Current**: teacher acts, students guess from desk.

**Upgrade — corner-running version**:
1. Tape 6 environment signs on **6 spots** around the classroom (4 corners + 2 walls work)
2. Teacher does the signature movement (from §5 above) WITHOUT saying which env
3. Students RUN to the matching environment sign
4. Last person to arrive (or anyone in the wrong spot) **does the movement once** as forfeit
5. Run 6 rounds (one per env)

**Sentence after each round**: 全班一起说 「老师在 ___!」 (K) or 「老师在 ___, 因为他/她在 ___」 (G1–3).

**New prompt**: For older students, let a *student* be the actor for round 7+. Other students guess.

### Bonus: 🏃 危险闪躲 Dodge the Danger (NEW, optional)

Quick 2-minute energizer slide before vocab section:

1. Teacher calls a danger ("熊!")
2. Class instantly does the matching reaction:
   - 熊 / 蛇 → 慢慢退后, 不跑 (back away slowly)
   - 蜜蜂 → 站住不动 (freeze)
   - 雷雨 → 蹲下, 远离大树 (squat, away from trees)
   - 沙尘暴 → 蹲下, 用衣服盖脸 (squat, cover face)
   - 河水太急 → 大喊「救命!」 (shout for help)
3. Students who do the wrong reaction sit out 1 round.

## 7. Sentence Frames — leveled & simpler

Replace the current sentence frames slide content with this exact set (matches user's spec):

### K / TK level (use these in slides 1–10 and on whole-class chants)

```
🟢 这是 ___ 。            (This is ___.)
🟢 很 ___ 。             (Very ___ — hot/cold/scary/bright)
🟢 我看到 ___ 。          (I see ___.)
🟢 安全 / 不安全          (Safe / not safe)
🟢 可以 / 不可以          (OK / not OK)
```

### G1 – G3 level (use these in interaction slides + reviews)

```
🟢 这里很 ___, 因为 ___ 。          (It's very ___ here, because ___.)
🟢 我应该 ___ 。                    (I should ___.)
🟢 ___ 不安全, 因为 ___ 。           (___ is not safe, because ___.)
🟢 我选 ___, 因为 ___ 。             (I choose ___, because ___.)
```

> **Print as a desk-card** (1 mini-poster per group, ~5 cards per class). Students refer to it during all activities. This is **0 prep** if printed once for the unit.

**Differentiation strategy**:
- TK / K: must produce 2-character chunk ("很热", "可以")
- G1: produce a 3–5 word sentence using 1 frame
- G2: produce 2 sentences linked with "因为"
- G3: produce 3-sentence "I see / I think / I choose" chain
